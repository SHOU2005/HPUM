      How it works

      1 · Data-quality audit

      The source data (Makaan.com listings, 6 metros) hides a serious defect: in rows where amenities were not recorded, prices are misaligned with their listings. Price vs area correlation is – there against – in recorded rows. These rows were removed.

        2 · Modelling

        • The model predicts log(price per sq ft), the way the Indian market quotes prices, then multiplies by area.
        • Localities are target-encoded with cross-fitting, so there is no leakage from the target.
        • Engineered features: log area, area per BHK, amenity count, and city.
        • Gradient Boosting and Extra Trees are tuned with randomised search, then stacked with a Ridge meta-model.
        • The 80% price range is calibrated on out-of-fold errors.

        3 · Limitations

        • These are asking prices from listings (about 2021), not registered sale prices. Adjust for appreciation since then.
        • Kolkata is excluded: only 75 trustworthy listings.
        • Localities missing from the data fall back to city-level pricing.
        • This is an indicative estimate, not a professional valuation.
